Real problems, the systems we built

Every project starts with one question: which process takes up most of the team's time? Here are three systems we designed to answer it.

[01] Trucking System
Logistics & group finance

One set of numbers for the whole group

Pain point
Each subsidiary's P&L was compiled by hand in Excel, separate from fleet operations data. As a result, group consolidation was prone to miscalculation.
Objective
Directors see the same, trustworthy numbers, from daily operations to the group report.
Solution
We merged two legacy systems into one platform. Monthly reports go through submission and approval before they are locked. Access is restricted per company, and every change is logged.
[02] Academic Information System
Education · tutoring center

Learning, teaching, and monitoring in one app

Pain point
Materials, grades, attendance, and teaching journals were scattered across many places. Teacher performance was hard to measure, and parents struggled to follow their child's progress.
Objective
One app for students, teachers, management, and parents, with teacher KPIs calculated from data.
Solution
Modules, practice quizzes, and scheduled try-outs for students. Photo check-ins and required journals for teachers. Scores flow into the KPIs automatically, and parents follow their child's progress charts.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find the answer you need? Ask our team directly.

What is Befisien Technologies?

Befisien Technologies is an IT and digital transformation consultancy in Indonesia. We help companies work more efficiently through AI agents, workflow automation, system integration, dashboards & apps, and content management services.

What is an AI agent, and how is it different from a regular chatbot?

A regular chatbot answers from predefined scripts. An AI agent understands questions in everyday language, draws its answers from company documents and data, and can carry out tasks such as creating tickets, updating data, or preparing reports.

Do we have to replace our existing systems?

Not necessarily. We usually start by connecting the systems you already use, such as your ERP, CRM, spreadsheets, or WhatsApp, and then automate the processes between them. We only recommend replacing a system when that is genuinely the better option.

Which industries can you help?

Our solutions are not limited to specific industries. Wherever there are repetitive processes, scattered data, or disconnected systems, we can help make them more efficient.

How long does a project take, and how much does it cost?

It depends on the scope and complexity of the process. After the audit stage, you receive a solution design with a written time and cost estimate before the project begins. The initial consultation is free.

What about the security of our company data?

Security requirements and data storage location are discussed from the design stage. Data access is limited to what the project needs, and the system can run on your company's own servers or cloud.

What happens after the system goes live?

We train your team, monitor system performance, and keep refining the solution as needed during the support stage.
